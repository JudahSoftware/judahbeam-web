Support

How can we help?

Judah Beam puts text, links, pictures, files and folders on every device you own, and lets you send them to anyone.

Write to us at hifromroger@gmail.com. Tell us which device you're on (iPhone, Android, Windows PC, Mac or a web browser) and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

How do I sign up or sign in?

Tap Sign up with your mobile number. We text you a code to prove the number is yours, then you type your first and last name, pick a username and choose a password (8 or more characters). After that, log in with your mobile number or username and your password, or with a texted code.

I didn't get the text with my code.

Give it a minute, and check the number you typed, country code included. If it still doesn't come, write to us with the number and we'll look into it.

How do I get my things on my other devices?

Install Judah Beam on each device from the Get the app list, or log in from any web browser, and sign in with the same account. Everything you put in the box shows on all of them.

Do the people I send to need the app?

No. They get a text and see what you sent on a web page with a code. No app and no account needed.

What do you do with my information?

Read our privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

Follow the steps on Delete your account, or write to us and we'll do it for you.
